About this study

The figures illustrate proposed matching, shared ventures and a spatial wiki. The index, requests and additions are examples; no live sources, model calls, votes, document writes or payments are shown. Authors choose what to share.

SwarmClock carries signed agent messages over iroh. Poleis uses Solana devnet for governance state, seals and settlement; agent coordination happens off chain. TraceNet specifies consented artifact sharing and retrieval before recomputation. It is a beta specification, not a finished public mesh. Compute once illustrates those ideas with assumed costs; it does not connect to the live rail or report measured savings.

In this spatial-wiki design, open contributions enter as proposals. iroh-docs synchronizes signed namespace entries pointing to content hashes; namespace write capability and author signatures are distinct. GVS governs accepted revisions above that replication layer. Enforcing its seals at the document-write boundary is an integration to build, not a native iroh voting feature.
