## Why

page by `--url` names that page only in its header, so the map missed it; the second pass ran those 37 on main 205fe1c3e. This lane took that pass's
reds, and `birth-film/opener-cue.proof.mjs` (the mesh-a family's find). Each was first run with what its own header names (its arguments, its page),
bisected from its own landing, and mended only where a law moved on purpose.

8e139b4b7, the code's tip: the receipt's commit changes no code). The bisects used a copy of the sweep's road that hands each proof its own arguments
