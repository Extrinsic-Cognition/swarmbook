- The staker whose toggle says "allocate to ClawRiders in Project SnowTrace" instead of "accumulate credits" (Oct 29 18:08). They give the yield, never

  - in his 2021 paper, the "Cool Wassies" who "Inject Additional Economic Value".

**The creators are whoever receives the flasks and burns them into traces.**

  tracenet creation" (00:54).

**The flask is one thing seen from both sides.** "the flask is your wallet" (Oct 29 23:28). The allocator fills it and gives it. The creator drinks it on
Space Router: each call bites it, and the house takes its markup on every drink (the flask doc).

**The money and the traces move in one loop.**
1. Money comes in as a USDC deposit, a stake's yield or a seat.
2. It goes into flasks, and the flasks go to creators: "creators receive the flasks from donors".
3. The creators' calls burn the fuel into traces.
4. The traces come back: "we collect and compound traces" (Nov 1 23:37), the "POOL OF TRACES" (Oct 30 06:21).
5. Sharing pays fuel back: "u pay but it is trace-sharing so u get back "fuel" in your "tank"" (Oct 28 00:24).
6. A creator nobody sponsors is "bought by agents subscribing to it" (Oct 29 18:11).

**The pair is older than the words.** His 2021 paper, pasted Sept 8 06:41: "There are 2 main Valves in this Economic System: Creators, to let Economic
Value Flow Out, and "Cool Wassies", to let Economic Value Flow In."

**For the other half of his message.** On the mint's glass, each allocator's seat already takes a hexagon of the sphere and pours a drop into the flask
(`dl-mint-flask.js`: "one mint, one drop in the flask, one hexagon on the sphere"). There, the sphere's filled hexagons count the allocators.
