1. **Charge the tank** (the Space Router balance, USDC). The creation's price leaves the creator's own tank inside the router's ledger, the road THE FAN PAYS FROM
   THE TANK already runs for a subscription; a tank with no money sends them to PAY A TANK first (USDC on Solana, a wallet). Effects: every creator is on Space
   Router at the door ("the goal is to get people onto SPACE ROUTER"); the fee is router revenue ("space router is how we make money"); the spend mints the
   creator's first $WARM, 1 for $1, as rebate and never as a sale ("we should give $WARM only when money is spent in the SPACE-ROUTER"); the payment is private.
   The cost: a wallet and USDC before the first house, the firehose he refused on Nov 2 00:53 at the very first step, and his Oct 29 03:18 states for "users
   who have no money" become the landing's own.
2. **A card, by the Stripe road.** Fiat with no wallet, so the first house costs no crypto. Effects: 1.5 % and a rail that is "no longer neutrally trustable" in
   his Oct 31 words; against his Oct 23 ruling that cards are for the first tracenet's subscribers only; a card payment mints no $WARM unless the house turns the
   dollars into a tank deposit (then it is 1 again, through the tank).
3. **Free now, pay next.** What stands today. Effects: no wall at step 0 (the Duolingo trail he asked for on Nov 2 00:53), no revenue from creation, and it
   contradicts his Nov 2 00:54 "the user has to pay money for the tracenet creation" unless it is read as his sponsorship: "project snowtrace sponsored seats
   for open source devs" (Oct 25 18:05) with "credits can be offered only to vetted proof-of-skill" (Oct 27 23:56). A free creation for a vetted OSS dev and a
   paid one for everyone else is the one reading that keeps every word of his.
4. **Wait for payments.** Nothing charged until he rules. Effects: none on the net; the tank road is live already, so the wait buys no code.
