## 6. The concept, in five lines

1. The Lyssajudron is a Goldberg ball: always twelve pentagons, the most it holds, and hexagons that grow ("Pentagons (Always 12) and Hexagons").
2. A pentagon is an agent. The unboxing lights three: the genie's three wishes, the trial of three AI calls.
3. A wish spent lights the next pentagon, then the next: they arise bottom up, in time, never laid in advance. The dark ones want fuel, $WARM.
4. Given a name, the Lyssajudron is your SWARMY: your companion, local AI, the Seeker's Muse, one per person, with a wallet, fuelled by $WARM.
5. So every party and board shows the pentagons that are lit, three to begin, twelve at most. Twelve is the budget, never the roster.
