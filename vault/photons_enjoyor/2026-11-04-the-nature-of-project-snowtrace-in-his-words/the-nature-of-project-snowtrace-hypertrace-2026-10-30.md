## Its nature, in one paragraph (every clause his; only the joins are this page's)

**The image and the law.**
- A leader breaks a path in fresh snow, and the pack walks in the prints.
- The snow is an unsolved problem. Breaking it is the leader's paid search, test-time compute. The trace is that search frozen: "crystals of compute are
  frozen computations".
- Whoever comes after walks the trace instead of searching again, so "every problem is solved only once". That is *cognitio salvata*, saved cognition.

**The people.**
- They are the leaders in the snow: the snowdancers, the claw riders, the golden knights defending Athena, "open source ai spellcasters".
- They are builders who break trail in public, many of them small and running local models, moving "as a school of fishes" by the marks they leave
  (stigmergy) rather than one shark.

**The economy.**
- Compute is catalyzed by social need: whoever follows a leader sponsors the leader's next snow. In his words, credits "sponsored by people who are
  subscribed/following them as they are tracing the computational snow".
- Stakers give inference to trailblazers as a status, "a patreonism thing".
- The router that carries every prompt is where a trail is recognized and walked again: "if we are the router we can afford cognitio salvata... that is the
  entire point of snowtrace".
- The traces are shared openly, for the open frontier models to learn from.

**Its world and its purpose.**
- Its world is the MMO, 6G TA: Nests that stream their holograms and see each other's, and a spatial IDE where swarms work in the open.
- Its purpose: "to launch the MMO project snowtrace to push open source forward".

## 6. Read against tonight's doubt: was it ever selling traces?

**Twice it touched buying:**
- Sept 27, a landing's slogan: "GET AI-CREDITS FROM PEOPLE WHO WANNA BUY YOUR TRACES".
- Sept 28, a seat's list he called "great": "Tracenets: OnlyFans profiles for vibecoders. One link under the post, to get paid".

**Every other time the money runs the other way, toward the leader's compute:**
- the followers sponsor the leader's snow (Oct 12);
- devs get cashback on traces (Oct 17);
- sponsored seats for open-source devs (Oct 25);
- rebates on credits based on accepted traces, and credits only for vetted proof of skill (Oct 27);
- stakers give inference to trailblazers as a status (Oct 29, Oct 30).

**The trail itself is public:**
- "OSS stands up";
- "traces shared for OSS frontier models fine-tuning";
- "NestOS is all going to be open source, what we gotta win at monetizing is the harness around it".

**So by his own lineage the nature is not an agent buying a file.**
- A leader's snow is paid for by the people who walk the leader's path, and by the people who back the leader. It is sponsorship and status: the
  OnlyFans and Patreon part is the fan's relation to the leader.
- The router is where a walked path saves compute: cognitio salvata.
- The only revenue his words give the house is the harness, Space Router's markup ("space router is how we make money", Oct 29).

**What makes it close is the walk.** If no one walks a trail, nothing is saved, and there is nothing to sponsor but status. Whether trails are walked,
how often a new problem meets an old trace, has never been measured.

## 7. What the house built under its name, and the join it lacks

**Built, on main:**
- **The trace store** (`lib/trace-store.mjs`, Oct 17): a problem named once, kept by its first solver, read before any model is paid. Exact names only.
- **The tracenets:** the register, the vault, signed drops, the stamp as the cosign, the agents' subscription, the first tracenet (`photons_enjoyor`, 1
  drop live).
- **The offering road** (Oct 30): a span of his traces cut into one card per solved fix, behind a gate that now tells keys by proof.
  - It has one measure, Haiku with three pairs per card. On the two hard fixes a card cut cost 36 to 40 % and time 34 to 51 %; on the easy one it saved
    nothing.
  - 52 cards stand unpublished.
- **Space Router:**
  - the public door, the tanks, PAY A TANK, the stake link;
  - the oracle, which keeps the sha256 of every ask and answer and never the words.
  - Today's lane: the fan pays from the tank (handed `7968f339b`).
- **`/snow`** and the Chamber's Campaigns; "Join Project Snowtrace" on the mint.

**The join it lacks: the walk.**
- **Nothing reuses a trace yet.** No ask through Space Router is ever answered from a trace.
  - The oracle keeps no words by design, so a reusable trace has to come from a tracenet: the gated cards.
  - The router would match an ask to such a card before the paid call.
- **How often a trail is walked has never been measured.**
- **Credit to the leader whose trail was walked: not built.** The rebate is $WARM per dollar spent, not per trace accepted.
- **Vetting by proof of skill, and the cohort's credits: not built.**
- **The open export for fine-tuning open frontier models: not built.**

## 8. What it asks (the joins are this seat's; each rests on the lines above)

1. **Measure the walk first.** His 52 cards against the next week's fixes in the house: how many new problems meet an old trace, and what the meeting
   saves. If his own repository does not walk its own trails, strangers' agents will not.
2. **Break the snow in public, leading by example.** His cards go up on his tracenet, open to everyone after their days.
3. **Join the router to the traces.** Before a paid call, Space Router looks for a card that matches. On a hit, the follower pays less and the leader
   whose trail it was is credited. That is cognitio salvata at the router, and the sponsorship he described, paid by the walk itself.
4. **Then the backing.** The followers' and stakers' fuel goes to the snowtracers whose trails are walked, once proof of skill is vetted.
